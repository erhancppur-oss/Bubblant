Bubblant – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Bubblant ("the app") is a casual game. This policy explains what data is processed when you play it.

Data the app itself stores

The app stores your game progress and settings (unlocked levels, stars, sound and language preferences) only on your device. This data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app uses your phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to steer the bubble. Sensor readings are used in real time on the device only and are not stored or transmitted.

The ant's voice uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Only the game's own pre-written phrases are spoken.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and approximate location, and information about your interactions with ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options.

Learn more about how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to content rated PG or lower.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on the project's GitHub page or contact the developer through the email address shown on the Google Play store listing.
