# Google Play store listing – Bubblant

## English (en-US) – default

**App name (≤30):** Bubblant: Tilt the Bubble

**Short description (≤80):**
Tilt your phone, float the bubble and carry the talking ant safely home!

**Full description:**
The whole screen is water. A tiny air bubble floats on it – and on top stands a very talkative ant who can't swim! Tilt your phone to steer the bubble through 120 maze levels and bring the ant safely to the lily pad.

But careful: hit a wall too fast and POP! – the bubble bursts and the ant goes glub glub…

🐜 A TALKING ANT
The ant tells you where to go: "Left!", "Up!", "Careful!!". Go the wrong way and it shakes its head. Keep ignoring it and it gets really angry! Do well and you get a thumbs up. Bring it home and it thanks you.

🌊 12 REAL-LIFE WORLDS, 120 LEVELS
Garden puddle, kitchen sink, bathtub, aquarium, garden pond, swimming pool, park fountain, rice paddy, tide pool, thunderstorm lake, storm drain and a flooded basement at night. Each one has its own dangers: the tap stream and the drain, rubber ducks, goldfish, hungry frogs, the pool robot, crabs, rats and something in the dark…

⏱️ SPECIAL CHALLENGES
• Rescue other ants stranded on leaves – they ride along on your bubble
• Leaking bubbles: grab O₂ bubbles before it shrinks away
• Races against the clock

🎵 MUSIC THAT GETS SCARIER
Cheerful tunes at the pond, adventure on the river, action in the storm and spine-chilling sounds in the abyss.

🎩 COLLECT & UNLOCK
Collect sugar cubes for stars and unlock hats for your ant: cap, bow, pirate hat, crown and wizard hat!

⚙️ YOUR WAY
Music, sound effects, the ant's voice and speech bubbles can each be turned off. Pick the ant's language and accent (e.g. American, British, Australian, Indian English; Spain or Mexico Spanish; Brazil or Portugal Portuguese) and its voice style. Touch control option, adjustable tilt sensitivity. Available in English, Türkçe, Español, Deutsch, Français and Português.

Free to play, supported by ads.
